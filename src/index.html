About Me

Hi, I'm Matthew McGrath, a California-based Full-Stack Web Developer with an obsession for beautiful, functional design.

about_matthew.md

## Origins & Foundations

I wrote my first program at 15, sparking a lifelong fascination with building things through code. That foundation led me to pursue Computer Science and spend years creating custom 3D game engines from scratch. As a solo developer, I designed, programmed, and published two commercial titles for the Xbox 360 Indie Games platform in C#.

## Modern Web Focus

Bringing that background to the web felt like a natural evolution. Today, my focus is building full-stack applications with React, Next.js, and Node.js, focusing on fast performance, clean design, and a great experience for real users.

## Systems to Web

Building game engines from scratch instilled a deep respect for memory, render cycles, and systems architecture. I bring that same low-level engineering discipline to modern web applications.

  • [x] Performance-first mindset — sub-second loads and zero layout shift
  • [x] Architectural rigor — clear boundaries from UI components to database schemas
  • [x] User-centric polish — thoughtful typography, accessibility, and micro-interactions

## Reliability & Mindset

Whether working independently or with a team, I believe clear communication, dependable follow-through, and thoughtful collaboration are just as essential as clean code. > “I build solutions meant to be read, maintained, and scaled with confidence.”
